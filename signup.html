Text START to (978) 637-8740

Text the keyword START to (978) 637-8740 to receive SMS alerts from Sarah Lachner Refurbished Product Availability Alerts.

What you receive

An automated text message when a product matching your saved specification is listed in Apple's public Certified Refurbished online store. Each alert names the part number, the configuration, the price, and a link to the product page. The application checks the store every 30 minutes and messages only when a newly matching listing appears.

Confirmation you will receive after texting START

Sarah Lachner Refurb Alert: You are now opted in to refurbished product availability alerts. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and is expected to be fewer than 5 messages per month. Messages are sent only when a matching listing appears.

Disclaimers

Message and data rates may apply. This service is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Apple Inc.

Help and opt out

Reply HELP to any message for help. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. Replying STOP ends all messages to that number.
